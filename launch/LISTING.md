## Description (under 2000 characters)

Copperbell Orchard is a five-reel, three-row slot with twenty lines and an original illustrated orchard. The wild is a tawny goat in a copper bell. Orchard lanterns, anywhere on the reels, open Lantern Watch: eight, ten, or twelve free spins. During the watch a bell bloom starts at 1× and grows by one after every winning spin, up to 3×.

There are two modes. Base is a 1× play and can open the watch on its own. Lantern starts the watch immediately and is played for 17.8×. Both modes return 96.33% over their cost. The maximum win in every mode is 5,000× the base amount, and it is present in the math books.

Nothing carries from one round to the next. Rules, the win table, each mode’s cost, the return, and the maximum win are in the info panel, with the general Engine disclaimer.
