## Tagline (under 400 characters)

A gentleman goat, an alpine vault, and tumbles that pile the loot.

## Description (under 2000 characters)

LE GOATY is an original 6-reel, 5-row cluster slot set in an alpine village. Five or more matching symbols touching by a side tumble away. The Goat is wild. Copper, silver and gold coins show a value, and the loot sack gathers every coin on the grid. Heist bells multiply a tumble. Winning cells can turn golden, and in Super and Epic those cells stay for the whole heist and rise through three glow levels.

Three Alpine Clovers open a Normal heist, four open Super, and five open Epic. Some three-clover heists reveal a deeper vault. Epic starts with golden cells already lit. Every heist waits for Start and ends on Collect. Ante, Normal, Super and Epic are separate modes beside the base game. The maximum win in every mode is 10,000× the base amount, and it is present in the math books.

Nothing carries from one round to the next. Rules, the win table, each mode’s cost, the return, and the maximum win are in the info panel, with the general Engine disclaimer.
