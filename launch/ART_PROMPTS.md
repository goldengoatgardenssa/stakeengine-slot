# Art direction

The game the reviewer plays is original vector ink: one cream tile, one ink outline, copper and moss fills, a slightly uneven stroke. Each symbol has its own idle motion and its own win motion. That art is in `game/src/symbols.js` and `game/styles.css`. It is not a set of stock icons.

The lobby images in this folder are painted orchard scenes and a keyed goat, made to the same palette so the tile stays bright on Stake’s dark background. They contain no words and no multipliers. If a reviewer still reads them as too smooth, replace them with new paintings and keep the vector game. Use these prompts and the same palette: ink `#2a2118`, cream `#f4e6c8`, copper `#c56a32`, moss `#2f6a45`, gold `#e7b256`, berry `#8d3a55`.

## Tile background, 3:4, no text

Bright gouache orchard in late morning, cream sky, blossom and amber fruit, one copper lantern on a branch, paper grain, pale corners, no letters, no animals, no people.

## Tile foreground, transparent

Adult tawny goat, three-quarter view, copper bell on a worn collar, one oak leaf by the ear, ink outline with a few broken strokes, gouache fur, no text, no ground, isolated.

## 16:9 cover and banner

Same orchard, a dirt path between two blossoming trees, high-key sky, the goat small enough to sit in the centre third so a crop still shows the bell.
